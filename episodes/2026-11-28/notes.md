Shashank started in twenty sixteen on a fifty thousand rupee monthly investment banking salary with a take home of forty nine thousand eight hundred rupees that he spent entirely, had only four lakh rupees in the bank when he married in February twenty twenty one, and reached his first crore in twenty twenty two. Today he earns about thirteen lakh rupees in a bad month and fifteen to twenty lakh in better months, from five sources: YouTube advertising at about three lakh fifty thousand rupees a month, brand deals, a content studio serving other companies, research analyst services at about five lakh rupees a month from around fifty customers a month, plus dividends and occasional real estate flips. His monthly spending totals eight lakh sixty two thousand rupees, but about five lakh of that is team salaries, a business cost; his own lines are rent of seventy thousand rupees (one lakh budgeted for his new place), loan payments of one lakh sixty four thousand rupees (a seventy two thousand home loan payment plus two car loans of ninety two thousand together), other household expenses of fifty thousand, and travel of fifty thousand, leaving a monthly surplus of five lakh rupees. His net worth is five crore forty seven lakh rupees: gross assets of six crore twenty three lakh against about seventy six lakh of loans (thirty five lakh home loan and forty one lakh car loans, roughly eleven to twelve percent of assets against his twenty percent comfort limit), with real estate of three crore fifteen lakh rupees across two houses, equities of about three crore (two crore eighty lakh in India and twenty two to twenty five lakh in the United States), gold of about eight lakh, and a separate eighteen lakh buy and hold portfolio for his young son. The cautionary lines are thirty lakh rupees across ten angel investments of three lakh each, nine of which failed, and ten to fifteen lakh spent repairing a second hand Mercedes he later gave away. The lessons are to never depend on one income stream, use debt deliberately and kill depreciating car loans first, track net worth monthly in a spreadsheet, and rent early while building income producing assets before buying a house, which he aims to do by age forty.
