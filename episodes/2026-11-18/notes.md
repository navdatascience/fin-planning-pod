## How I ACTUALLY Spend My 82 Lakh Salary in Warangal — PerspectiveByICM

**Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61OZyLSCjZA

**Spreadsheet:** `spreadsheets/V002_61OZyLSCjZA_How-I-ACTUALLY-Spend-My-82-Lakh-Salary-in-Warangal.xlsx`

Jitender, a software engineer in Warangal, Telangana supporting a family of five, combines a Rs 50 lakh salary with Rs 31 lakh of freelance income and Rs 1.8 lakh of rent from a commercial property. His take-home is Rs 4,21,867 a month; he spends Rs 1,12,100 (Rs 65,600 responsible, Rs 46,500 bonus) and invests Rs 1,88,600 a month — Rs 1 lakh in SIPs, plus equities, fixed deposits, gold, and SSY for his daughter — keeping surplus cash to deploy lump-sum on market falls. His portfolio totals Rs 2.05 crore. Lessons: do not depend on salary alone, split investments into clear buckets, keep dry powder for market falls, and let freelancing raise the investable surplus.
