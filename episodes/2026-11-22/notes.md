## How I ACTUALLY Spend My 38 Lakh Salary in Maharashtra — PerspectiveByICM channel

**Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tWWyU_7eAQQ

**Spreadsheet:** `spreadsheets/V008_tWWyU_7eAQQ_How-I-ACTUALLY-Spend-My-38-Lakh-Salary-in-Maharashtra.xlsx`

An unnamed software developer in Maharashtra, working on contract with no full-time job, married with a child aged two, earns Rs 38 lakh a year — Rs 35 lakh base salary and Rs 3 lakh from playbook sales. His take-home is Rs 2,64,206 a month. He spends Rs 79,522 a month — Rs 45,922 responsible (including Rs 15,000 groceries, Rs 9,000 essentials, and Rs 5,672 across six insurance policies covering cancer care, accident, critical illness, renter's, family floater, and individual health cover) and Rs 33,599 bonus, with subscriptions the largest part at Rs 24,000 — and pays no rent through a Rs 5 lakh heavy-deposit lease. He invests Rs 1,00,300 a month: Rs 1,00,000 into direct equities through an ETF 'Lego strategy' spanning the whole Indian market, Rs 1,800 in EPF, and Rs 300 in REITs and InvITs, leaving a monthly surplus of Rs 84,384. He has made Rs 5,18,000 of one-off big spends. His portfolio totals Rs 14,41,761, including Rs 6 lakh in equities, Rs 5 lakh in mutual funds, and Rs 3,41,761 in EPF and related balances, with no direct real estate or gold. Lessons: build a diversified ETF portfolio instead of picking stocks, use REITs and InvITs for property exposure without buying property, over-insure after seeing family medical costs, and keep idle money in money-market funds rather than a low-interest savings account.
