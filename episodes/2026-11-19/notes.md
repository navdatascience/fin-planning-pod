## How I ACTUALLY Spend My ₹41,60,000 Salary — PerspectiveByICM channel

**Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BfdLa9Z_ft0

Pavit, a data and AI specialist in Bangalore supporting a family with a three-month-old daughter, earns Rs 41.6 lakh a year — Rs 36 lakh base salary, Rs 3.6 lakh variable pay, and a Rs 2 lakh joining bonus. His take-home is Rs 2,18,800 a month; he and his wife split shared costs through a joint account (each contributes over Rs 50,000 a month) while investments stay individual. He spends Rs 1,08,550 a month — Rs 84,400 responsible (including Rs 46,000 of loan payments: a Rs 31,000 home-loan share and a Rs 15,000 education loan) and Rs 24,150 bonus — and invests Rs 92,000 a month: Rs 75,000 in SIPs, Rs 12,000 in EPF, PPF and NPS, and Rs 5,000 in an RD building a Rs 5 lakh emergency fund. His portfolio totals Rs 80.75 lakh, including Rs 40 lakh in real estate, Rs 12 lakh in mutual funds, and Rs 7.5 lakh in equities, and he has made an Rs 8.5 lakh one-off big spend. Lessons: invest first and spend the rest, split shared couple expenses through a joint account, step up SIPs as income grows, and defend and protect with insurance and an emergency fund before growing.
